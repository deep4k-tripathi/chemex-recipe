    Dialing it in

    • Brew too sour or weak? Grind finer, pour slower, or bump coffee up.
    • Brew too bitter or harsh? Grind coarser, pour faster, or drop the dose.
    • Total brew time is the north star — 4:00 to 4:30 for this dose.

    Tips for a noticeably better cup

    • Weigh everything. Volume measurements drift; weight doesn't.
    • Use freshly ground coffee — ideally within 2 weeks of roast date, ground just before brewing.
    • Filtered water matters more than people think. Skip distilled; use spring or filtered tap.
    • The Chemex spout wants to pour clean and slow — a gooseneck kettle helps, but you can manage with a regular kettle by tilting carefully.

    The 1:16 ratio (coffee to water) is a solid starting point. Once you nail this, try 1:15 for a stronger cup or 1:17 for a lighter, tea-like brew.
